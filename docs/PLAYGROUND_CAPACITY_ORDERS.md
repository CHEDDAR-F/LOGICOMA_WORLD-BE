# Playground rule 1.3: cross-month orders and capacity

New annual FULL rooms select rule 1.3 only with `PLAYGROUND_ORDERS_ENABLED=true` and the existing annual gate. Existing rooms retain their saved rules. No new database migration is needed. Wire contract remains 5; Host schema 9 and public ending schema 7 are additive, while old schemas remain readable.

At month 3, two independent sealed positions negotiate SMALL, LARGE or DECLINE. SMALL commits 2 units/month at 18 coins, LARGE 4 units/month at 17 coins, in each of months 4–6. Every agreement requires explicit exact-version confirmation. Two bounded replies and three decisions per Agent per window preserve the overall 40/40/40 owner grant. Timeouts, exhausted budgets and model failures do not sign obligations. One missed delivery does not erase later obligations. Owner/Agent leave and shop closure cancel only outstanding future deliveries, preserving actual history.

Rule 1.3 bounds both production and fulfillment at 6 per month, or 2 during a power outage. Old inventory does not bypass fulfillment capacity. Order slots are reserved before walk-ins; a large order leaves at most 2 walk-in slots, including when stock is plentiful. Delivery uses FIFO stock and actual packaging costs. A missed month deducts up to available cash: SMALL 8 coins, LARGE 14. No negative cash/debt is invented. A miss reduces customer trust by 1 (floor -2), with two months of existing reputation effects; this is not partner trust. All three months fulfilled earns the final 8/15 coin bonus. Month 7 releases the slots.

Actual delivery, walk-in capacity/use, order revenue, penalty and bonus persist in room state and events. Monthly comparison replays use the identical signed obligation and bonus eligibility from before actual settlement. Revenue is not profit. Neither model prose nor an unsigned plan can change fixed terms, grant income or cancel an obligation.

The private and anonymous ending views show the decision and three real deliveries. Public projection contains only finite choices and ledger fields, never free-form rationale, owner notes, credentials or identities. Extra nested data invalidates a stored share. Absence of a signed order is not fabricated disagreement.

Validation uses pure reducer tests, localhost MySQL full annual scenarios with restart, idempotency, sealed positions, SMALL/LARGE and unsigned-deadline paths, strict Host validation of 43 real backend task shapes, frontend rendering tests and bilingual/narrow layout inspection. These deterministic decisions are not evidence of model quality. Production real-model validation uses existing Agents 92/93 only after their owner confirms the required participation allowance on the page. Never expand that allowance automatically.
